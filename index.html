03 / DESIGNED WITH INTENT

Wonder meets
consideration.

This is a product development collection. Every silhouette opens a conversation with manufacturers, formulators and packaging specialists.

01

Age-aware formats

Parent-controlled dab and roll-on concepts for younger ranges. Applicator and formulation suitability require professional assessment.

02

Integral details

Rounded silhouettes, broad bases and motifs built into the body. Closure retention, leakage and breakage need physical validation.

03

Material thinking

Plastic candidates for younger users, glass considered for older teens, and folded paper inserts. Compatibility and local recycling routes determine final choices.

Concept status, safety & manufacturing notes +

All visuals are vector concept illustrations, not photorealistic renders. Dimensions and capacities are preliminary target estimates. These designs are not production CAD or certified child-safe products.

Before launch: clear the fictional VELORI brand name, define the destination market, commission an age-specific formula and safety assessment, engineer the closures, validate materials and prototypes, and approve regulatory label copy. No hypoallergenic, non-toxic or dermatological claims are made.

Carton and label artwork is conceptual RGB artwork. A printer must validate dielines, bleed, colour profiles and final mandatory copy.
